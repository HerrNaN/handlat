# Vision

Handlat makes everyday grocery shopping easier with shared lists and personal recipes that become shopping lists.

It is built for its creator, friends, and family: useful in daily life, enjoyable to develop, and manageable to maintain.

## Goals

Simple. Shared. Personal.

- Keep everyday shopping quick and clear on a phone.
- Let people prepare and share lists, with changes appearing in real time.
- Let people keep their own recipes and add their ingredients to shopping lists.
- Help people use recipes they already have, wherever those recipes come from.
- Collect only the information needed to provide the application; avoid unnecessary personal details and tracking.
- Make the web the primary home, accessible on phones and computers.
- Offer a home-screen app experience without requiring app-store distribution or an Apple or Google account to access Handlat.
- Keep implementation and ongoing maintenance proportional to a small, self-hosted project.
- Leave room to learn, experiment, and enjoy building the application.
- In matters of taste or ambiguity, favor simplicity…
- …but accept complexity when it makes everyday use meaningfully better.

## Non-goals

- Commercial growth, monetization, or persuading people to leave tools they already like.
- Collecting personal information beyond what the application needs.
- Publishing and maintaining native apps.
- Making basic list use depend on recipe management.
- Adding extensive caching or a full offline architecture without a concrete need.

## Project direction

Shared shopping lists are the foundation. Personal recipes are the natural extension.

Website imports and extracting recipes from photographs or scans are possible ways to reduce manual entry. They are ideas to explore, not commitments to particular features, technologies, or delivery dates.

This charter guides decisions; it is not a feature inventory or a fixed roadmap. Contributors and agents should explain how proposed changes serve these goals and justify the complexity they introduce.
